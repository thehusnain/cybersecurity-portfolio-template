# Spider sprite

`jumping-spider.png` is a transparent 1254 × 1254 sprite made with the built-in image generation tool for this portfolio. Runtime canvas slices it into the body, head and eight articulated legs. This is a custom adaptation; the Found source HTML and its embedded photographs were not supplied. The fly, expressions and silk are procedural canvas drawings.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent animation sprite. Square macro orthographic TOP DOWN dorsal photograph of a brown and cream banded jumping spider facing DOWN. Abdomen centered at 50%,29%, waist 50%,44%, head 50%,58%. Eight symmetrically spread, separate hairy legs, with 5% margins. Front pair: hips 37/63%,61%, knees 29/71%,69%, feet 32/68%,93%. Next pair: hips 37/63%,54%, knees 24/76%,56%, feet 8/92%,77%. Next pair: hips 38/62%,49%, knees 29/71%,46%, feet 5/95%,38%. Rear pair: hips 39/61%,45%, knees 29/71%,34%, feet 33/67%,5%. Truly transparent background, no ground, no shadows, no web, no text. Sharp macro texture, soft diffuse illumination.
